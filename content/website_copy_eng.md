## My Journey Section

### Section Headline
The backwards path

### Section Intro
Most PMs study product management. I took the long way: design, code, and only then product. It was uncomfortable, exhausting, full of mistakes. And that's exactly why I actually know what I'm doing today.

### Timeline Phase 1: 2012-2018
**Title**: Designer & Business Owner @ Selfrules

**What I did**: Designed websites. Managed clients. Thought I knew everything.

**What I learned**: I founded an agency and crashed into business reality. I learned that beautiful design nobody knows how to use is just art, not product. That a perfect mockup that can't be developed is wasted time. And that saying "no" to the wrong client is worth more than a thousand mediocre projects.

**The failure that counts**: Spending 3 weeks on a design only to discover it was technically impossible within budget. That's when I decided: I need to learn to code.

### Timeline Phase 2: 2016-2020
**Title**: Full-stack Developer @ FLOWING

**What I did**: Wrote code. Built APIs. Thought tech solved everything.

**What I learned**: I spent nights optimizing an algorithm to make it 10ms faster, while the real problem was that nobody understood how to use the feature. I wrote the most elegant code of my career for a product that died after 4 months. I learned that perfect code nobody uses is useless code.

**The turning point**: A PM colleague asked me: "But what is this really for?". I couldn't answer. I had built a solution looking for a problem. Never again.

### Timeline Phase 3: 2021-2023
**Title**: Product Owner @ ActiveProspect

**What I did**: Bridged business and tech. Translated needs into features.

**What I learned**: That the real job of Product isn't saying "yes" to everything, but saying "no" to the right things. That a beautiful roadmap means nothing if it's not aligned with business. And that user feedback matters more than any internal stakeholder's opinion (mine included).

**The skill I acquired**: Speaking three languages fluently - designer language, developer language, business language - and translating between them in real-time.

### Timeline Phase 4: 2023-present
**Title**: Product Manager @ QubicaAMF

**What I do**: Unify everything I learned to build things that matter.

**The results**: -12% payment times (not magic, just fewer useless clicks). +9% integration adoption (less PDF, more 3-minute videos). -25% post-release incidents (putting Product, Support, and Engineering in the same room every Friday).

**Why it works**: I can prototype an idea in the morning, write API specs at lunch, and present the business case in the afternoon. Not because I'm a genius, but because I've done all these jobs. I know where the gaps are. I know what can go wrong. And I know how to prevent it.

## Work Together Section

### Section Headline
How we can work together

### Section Intro
I don't sell consulting. I don't sell hours. I sell results. If you have a concrete problem and want someone who understands design, tech, and business without needing translators, we can talk.

### Mode 1: Strategic consultations
**Headline**: Unblock your product in 90 minutes

**Description**: Got a product that won't take off? A team spinning in circles? A roadmap that looks strategic but delivers no results? Let's take 90 minutes. I'll ask you the uncomfortable questions nobody's ever asked. We'll analyze together where you're stuck. And we'll come out with a concrete action plan.

**Don't expect**: Beautiful slides with buzzwords.

**Expect**: Direct questions, pragmatic analysis, clear action plan. And probably some truths you won't like hearing (but need to).

**Ideal for**: Founders, Product Managers, Tech Leads who know they're stuck but don't understand where.

### Mode 2: Brainstorming sessions
**Headline**: Two brains, one problem, infinite solutions

**Description**: Sometimes the problem isn't that you don't have solutions. It's that you have too many and don't know which to choose. Or worse, you have the wrong solution to the right problem.

Let's take 2 hours. I bring the experience of someone who got design, development, and product wrong. You bring your problem. Together we explore possibilities you wouldn't see alone.

**What we do**: Practical work session. Sketches, diagrams, cost-benefit analysis. We come out with solution prototypes, not just vague ideas.

**Ideal for**: Teams in discovery phase, complex architectural decisions, when you need an outsider to challenge your assumptions.

### Mode 3: Mentorship
**Headline**: The path I wish someone had shown me

**Description**: Are you a designer who wants to understand development? A developer who wants to move to product? A junior PM who wants to grow?

I've been there. Without guidance. With all possible mistakes. I can save you time, money, and frustration by showing you the patterns that work and the mistakes to avoid.

**Format**: 1 hour every 2 weeks. Analysis of your current work. Practical advice. Access to templates and frameworks I use. And most importantly: the right questions to ask yourself before every decision.

**Not for you if**: You're looking for someone to tell you what to do. I'm looking for people who want to learn to think, not executors.

**For you if**: You want a personalized path with someone who truly understands where you're coming from and where you want to go.
