## 1. Current State Analysis

The current brand story is distributed across multiple touchpoints on selfrules.org, with the hero section as the primary narrative vehicle:

#### Hero Section (First Impression)

**Opening Hook:**
> "I failed as a designer. Then as a developer. Now I'm the PM you call when everyone says 'yes' but nobody knows what to do."

**Problem Setup:**
> "Why? Because after 13 years of expensive mistakes I learned one thing: the problem is never what they tell you in the first meeting."

**Call to Action:**
> "Let's talk" / "How I got here"

#### Journey Section (Full Story Arc)

The story continues through a career timeline:
1. **Selfrules (2012-2018)** - Founder, €8K failure story
2. **FLOWING (2016-2020)** - Designer/Developer, learned to code
3. **ActiveProspect (2020-2023)** - Product Owner, became the bridge
4. **QubicaAMF (2023-present)** - PM, unifies everything learned

#### Closing Statement:
> "13 years becoming a generalist. Specialization makes you good. Cross-pollination makes you useful."

## 2. Key Story Elements to Preserve

The following elements are **working well** and must be protected in any story rewrite:

### PRESERVE #1: Failure-First Opening

**Why It Works:**
- Counter-intuitive hook cuts through "thought leader" noise
- Creates immediate trust through vulnerability
- Memorable and shareable
- Differentiates from competitors who lead with credentials

**Current Text (Keep):**
> "I failed as a designer. Then as a developer."

**Evidence of Effectiveness:**
- Competitors (Lenny, Shreyas, Marty, Teresa, Melissa) all lead with success
- Research shows vulnerability increases trust and likability
- Matches brand voice (Failure-Forward attribute)

**Preservation Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL - Do not remove or soften

---

### PRESERVE #2: The €8K Failure Story

**Why It Works:**
- Specific financial cost makes it believable
- "Beautiful but confusing" insight is quotable and teachable
- Proves skin in the game
- Creates emotional connection through shared pain

**Current Text (Keep):**
> "'Beautiful, but where do I click to pay?' Zero sales for 3 weeks. That project cost me €8K in refunds and 2 months of burned reputation."

**Preservation Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL - This is the brand's signature story

---

### PRESERVE #3: The "Three Languages" Insight

**Why It Works:**
- Crystallizes the unique value proposition
- Creates vivid mental image
- Easy to remember and repeat
- Backed by career trajectory proof

**Current Text (Keep):**
> "My superpower today? I speak three languages fluently:
> - Design Language: 'The user gets stuck here because the visual hierarchy is wrong'
> - Developer Language: 'This feature would require 3 sprints of refactoring'
> - Business Language: 'This problem costs EUR15K/month in support tickets'
> You don't need a translator when you are the translator."

**Preservation Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL - Core mechanism articulation

---

### PRESERVE #4: Specific Quantified Results

**Why It Works:**
- Proof points build credibility
- Specific numbers are believable and memorable
- Shows real-world impact, not theory

**Current Metrics (Keep):**
- Payment times: -12%
- Post-release incidents: -25%
- Integration adoption: +9%
- Top-ups in one day: +30
- Uptime/success rate: ≥99%
- Sprints saved: 2 (from 30-min call)

**Preservation Priority:** 🟠 HIGH - Always include in proof bundles

---

### PRESERVE #5: Anti-Corporate Voice

**Why It Works:**
- Creates contrast with typical consulting language
- Builds authenticity
- Appeals to target audience skeptical of fluff

**Current Examples (Keep):**
- "No 'Thrilled to announce' or 'Grateful for this opportunity'. Just facts."
- "Not slides. Actions."
- "Monday, not 'by Q2'"
- "Zero pitch. Zero slides. Just your problem"

**Preservation Priority:** 🟠 HIGH - Core voice attribute

---

### PRESERVE #6: Core Problem Articulation

**Why It Works:**
- Vivid, relatable, specific
- Visitors self-identify immediately
- Creates urgency

**Current Text (Keep):**
> "Got 4 features in development and none finished? Designer and developer not talking?"
> "Roadmap full but no priorities? Designer and developer not talking?"
> "Feature requests from 7 different stakeholders. All urgent. All 'strategic'."

**Preservation Priority:** 🟠 HIGH - Problem articulation is the strongest positioning element

## 3. Key Story Elements to Strengthen

The following elements need **optimization** to maximize story impact:

### STRENGTHEN #1: Category Claim (Missing → Explicit)

**Current State:**
The "Translator" metaphor appears throughout but is never claimed as a category identity. No "I am a _____" statement exists.

**Problem:**
- Visitors may categorize as generic "PM consultant" or "freelancer"
- Unique positioning is hidden, not displayed
- Competitors could adopt similar language without pushback

**Strengthening Required:**
Transform implicit metaphor into explicit category ownership:

| Current | Strengthened |
|---------|--------------|
| "I translate" (verb, scattered) | "I'm a **Discipline Translator**" (noun, claimed) |
| No category statement | "I'm not a consultant. I'm a Discipline Translator." |
| Metaphor explained in Journey | Category claimed in Hero |

**Strengthening Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL

---

### STRENGTHEN #2: Hero Problem Statement (Prominent → Proven)

**Current State:**
> "The problem is never what they tell you in the first meeting"

**Problem:**
- This is the brand's second impression (hero subtitle)
- No supporting evidence is visible nearby
- Reads as claim, not proven insight

**Strengthening Required:**
Either (a) add proof for this claim or (b) replace with better-evidenced promise.

**Option A: Add Proof**
> "The problem is never what they tell you in the first meeting. Like when a team told me 'our developers are slow'—turns out their specs had 47 undefined edge cases."

**Option B: Replace with Proven Claim**
> "The problem is that your designer says 'user journey,' your developer says 'technical debt,' and nobody's building the same product. I translate."

**Strengthening Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL

---

### STRENGTHEN #3: Target Audience (Vague → Specific)

**Current State:**
Implied multiple audiences (founders, PMs, tech leads, designers) with no explicit ICP.

**Problem:**
- Attracts mismatched leads
- Dilutes positioning clarity
- Makes conversion messaging harder

**Strengthening Required:**
Add explicit target audience statement to story:

| Current | Strengthened |
|---------|--------------|
| (None) | "I work with mid-to-senior PMs at growth-stage tech companies (50-500 people)..." |
| Multiple roles implied | Primary ICP explicit + "Who I'm NOT For" filter |

**Strengthening Priority:** 🔴 CRITICAL

---

### STRENGTHEN #4: Transformation Promise (Dispersed → Concentrated)

**Current State:**
The transformation promise ("build the right thing, not just something") appears:
- In footer bio
- In services section
- In testimonials

But NOT in the hero story arc.

**Problem:**
- Promise is discovered, not delivered upfront
- Story ends on mechanism, not outcome
- CTAs lack clear benefit

**Strengthening Required:**
Move transformation promise earlier in story:

| Current Story Flow | Strengthened Flow |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Failure → Journey → Mechanism → Offer | Failure → Mechanism → **Promise** → Proof → Offer |
| Promise in Footer | Promise in Hero |

**Strengthening Priority:** 🟠 HIGH

---

### STRENGTHEN #5: Story Continuity (Linear → Integrated)

**Current State:**
Story starts in Hero, continues in Journey section, with different voice/format.

**Problem:**
- Visitors who don't scroll miss the full narrative
- Story feels like two pieces, not one arc
- Hero story is incomplete without Journey

**Strengthening Required:**
Create a complete mini-story in hero that doesn't require Journey section:

| Current | Strengthened |
|---------|--------------|
| Hero = Hook + Teaser | Hero = Complete Story Arc (Hook → Problem → Mechanism → Promise → Proof → CTA) |
| Journey = Full Story | Journey = Deep Dive (Optional) |

**Strengthening Priority:** 🟠 HIGH

---

### STRENGTHEN #6: Competitive Differentiation (Implicit → Explicit)

**Current State:**
Career path proves unique cross-functional capability, but differentiation from competitors is never stated.

**Problem:**
- Visitors may not realize this positioning is unique
- "Why Mattia vs. Shreyas/Lenny/etc." question unanswered
- Value proposition competes on implicit features, not explicit positioning

**Strengthening Required:**
Add explicit differentiation statement:

| Current | Strengthened |
|---------|--------------|
| (Implied by career) | "Unlike PM coaches who teach frameworks, I translate between the people who use them." |
| (None) | "Other PM consultants speak business. I speak business, design, AND code." |

**Strengthening Priority:** 🟡 MEDIUM

## 4. Rewritten Brand Story

### The Complete Rewritten Story

This rewritten story can be used as the primary brand narrative across touchpoints. It preserves all high-performing elements while strengthening identified gaps.

## **The Story of the Discipline Translator**

### Opening Hook (Preserve: Failure-Forward)

**I failed as a designer.**

My first project as a 25-year-old agency founder? A "stunning" e-commerce interface. My first client feedback? *"Beautiful, but where do I click to pay?"*

Zero sales for 3 weeks. €8K in refunds. Two months of burned reputation.

**Then I failed as a developer.**

Three weeks spent designing something technically impossible. The client canceled. I learned the hard way: a perfect mockup that can't be built is just expensive wallpaper.

---

### The Insight (Strengthen: Mechanism)

Here's what 13 years of expensive mistakes taught me:

**The problem is never design. The problem is never development. The problem is never business.**

The problem is translation.

Your designer says *"user journey."*
Your developer says *"technical debt."*
Your CFO says *"just ship something."*

Same meeting. Three different languages. Zero alignment.

Most PMs only speak business. They join these meetings and watch chaos unfold. They say "yes" because they can't translate "no."

I speak all three languages fluently—**because I've failed in all three.**

---

### The Category Claim (Strengthen: New)

**I'm not a PM consultant. I'm a Discipline Translator.**

That's not just a title. It's what happens when you've spent 6 years in design and development, 3 years bridging business and tech as a Product Owner, and now lead product at a company where digital products impact physical experiences.

I don't teach frameworks. I translate between the people who use them.

---

### The Promise (Strengthen: Explicit)

What does that mean for you?

**Your team builds the right thing. Not just something. Not "by Q2." Monday.**

A solution your designer understands, your developer can build, and your CFO approves. No translator in between—because you'll be talking to one.

---

### The Proof (Preserve: Evidence)

*"We spent 6 months building features nobody used. One 90-minute session with Mattia and we finally understood what our users actually needed. Three weeks later, we shipped the right thing."*
— Sarah Chen, Founder & CEO, TechFlow AI

*"My team spoke three different languages: design wanted beauty, engineering wanted clean code, business wanted revenue. Mattia translated all three. Finally, we're building the same product."*
— Marco Bianchi, Product Manager, FinanceHub

The numbers behind the translation:
- **-12%** payment processing times
- **-25%** post-release incidents
- **+30** top-ups sold in a single Sunday
- **2 sprints** saved from a 30-minute call

---

### The Audience (Strengthen: Explicit)

**Who I work with:**
Mid-to-senior PMs and product leaders at growth-stage tech companies (50-500 people). If your designer says "user journey" while your developer says "technical debt" and you're stuck in the middle—we should talk.

**Who I'm NOT for:**
Early-stage founders hunting for product-market fit. Enterprise teams with 6-month procurement cycles. People looking for someone to execute specs without questioning them.

---

### The Closing (Preserve: Voice + Strengthen: CTA)

Still here?

Good. That means the chaos is real and the spreadsheets aren't solving it.

**15-minute call. Zero pitch. Zero slides. Just your problem and how to unblock it.**

Monday morning, you'll know exactly what to build.

[**Let's talk →**]
