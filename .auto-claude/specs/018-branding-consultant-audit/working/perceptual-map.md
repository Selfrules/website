# Perceptual Map Analysis: PM Personal Brand Landscape

**Date:** January 27, 2026
**Task:** Subtask 3-3 - Create 2x2 perceptual map with competitors plotted
**Purpose:** Visualize competitive positioning and identify white space opportunities for selfrules.org

---

## Executive Summary

This perceptual map positions selfrules.org (Mattia De Luca) against 5 key competitors in the PM/tech personal brand space. The analysis reveals a significant **white space opportunity** in the "Practical + Personal" quadrant—a market position no major competitor currently owns.

**Key Finding:** While competitors cluster around "Theoretical/Scalable" (thought leadership) or "Practical/Enterprise" (org transformation), Mattia's positioning as a **hands-on practitioner offering boutique services** is genuinely differentiated and defensible.

## White Space Analysis

### Primary White Space: "The Practitioner's Coach"

**Location:** High Practical (7-9) × High Personal (1-3)

**Why This Space Is Empty:**

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Economics** | 1:1 practical work is time-intensive and hard to scale. Competitors optimize for scale (courses, content, enterprise). |
| **Expertise barrier** | Requires actual practitioner experience (not just thought leadership). Must "have done it" recently, not decades ago. |
| **Brand positioning** | Being "practical and personal" sounds less prestigious than "thought leader." Competitors prefer authority positioning. |
| **Market visibility** | Boutique providers don't have 900K audiences, so they're less visible. The space exists but is hidden. |

**Why Mattia Wins This Space:**

| Advantage | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| **Practitioner credibility** | Currently active as PM at QubicaAMF (not retired to advisory). Recent failures and wins documented. |
| **Cross-functional depth** | Worked as designer, developer, and PM. Can give practical advice across disciplines. |
| **Service model fit** | 90-min sessions, bi-weekly mentorship, brainstorming workshops. Designed for personal engagement. |
| **Authentic positioning** | "I failed as a designer. Then as a developer." → Practitioner who learned by doing, not teaching. |

## Strategic Implications

### 1. Own the "Practical + Personal" Quadrant

**Action:** Explicitly claim this positioning in messaging.

**Recommended Language:**
> "While thought leaders write about product, I work with you on your product. While course creators teach thousands, I partner with individuals. The problem isn't that you don't know what to do—it's that you're drowning in chaos and need someone in your corner."

**Supporting Proof Points:**
- 90-minute 1:1 strategic consulting
- Bi-weekly mentorship with real project review
- "Monday morning decisions, not quarterly slides"

---

### 2. Position Against the Quadrant Neighbors

**Contrast with Thought Leaders (Shreyas, Marty):**
> "Shreyas will tell you *how to think* about product. I'll tell you *what to do Monday*."

**Contrast with Mass Trainers (Lenny, Teresa):**
> "Lenny's newsletter teaches you frameworks. I help you apply them to your specific chaos—this week."

**Contrast with Strategy Consultants (Melissa):**
> "Melissa transforms organizations. I unblock individuals."

---

### 3. Claim "Discipline Translator" as Category

**The Opportunity:** No competitor occupies the cross-functional translation space.

**Category Claim Language:**
> "I'm not a PM consultant. I'm a Discipline Translator—the person you call when your designer says 'user journey' while your developer says 'technical debt' and your CFO says 'just ship something.' I speak all three languages."

**Defensibility:** This claim requires:
- Actual experience in design, development, AND PM
- Active practice (not retired to advisory)
- Evidence of cross-functional work

Competitors cannot credibly make this claim.

---

### 4. Leverage the "Empty Quadrant" Advantage

**Strategic Benefits of the Practical + Personal Position:**

| Benefit | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| **No direct competition** | Pricing power, differentiation without comparison |
| **Higher engagement value** | 1:1 relationships create testimonials, referrals, case studies |
| **Lower marketing cost** | Quality over quantity—fewer but better-fit clients |
| **Sustainable practice** | Not competing for attention with 900K-follower players |

**Risk to Monitor:** If the position works, bigger players may expand into it. Defensibility comes from:
- Authentic practitioner positioning (they can't fake recent hands-on work)
- Cross-functional career history (they can't replicate 13 years overnight)
- Boutique brand (they're invested in scale models)
