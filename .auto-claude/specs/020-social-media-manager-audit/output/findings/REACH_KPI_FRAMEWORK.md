**Framework:** REACH Metrics Model (Reach, Engagement, Amplification, Conversion, Holding)

---

## Executive Summary

This document defines the **REACH Metrics Framework** for measuring social media success for selfrules.org. The framework prioritizes **community quality over vanity metrics**, aligning with the B2B professional services business model. Each metric is defined with platform-specific targets, measurement methods, and actionable benchmarks.

### REACH Framework Overview

```
THE REACH METRICS MODEL
===============================================================================

  R ─────► E ─────► A ─────► C ─────► H
  │        │        │        │        │
  ▼        ▼        ▼        ▼        ▼
REACH   ENGAGE   AMPLIFY  CONVERT   HOLD

Unique   Quality   Organic  Profile   Dwell
viewers  interact  shares   visits    time
         (NOT      reposts  clicks    retention
          likes)            bookings

===============================================================================

Business Value Flow:
REACH (Awareness) → ENGAGEMENT (Resonance) → AMPLIFICATION (Distribution)
→ CONVERSION (Action) → HOLDING (Loyalty)

```

### Anti-Vanity Metrics Commitment

| Vanity Metric (AVOID) | Community Metric (TRACK) | Why |
|-----------------------|--------------------------|-----|
| Follower count | Active commenters per post | Followers ≠ customers |
| Total impressions | Quality reach to target personas | Impressions ≠ engagement |
| Likes count | Comment depth & quality | Likes = low effort, no intent |
| Post views | Profile-to-booking ratio | Views ≠ interest |
| Connection count | Meaningful DM conversations | Connections ≠ relationships |

## REACH Metric #1: REACH (R)

### Definition

**Reach** measures the **unique accounts who saw your content**. It represents awareness and top-of-funnel visibility. However, raw reach is meaningless without context—we focus on **qualified reach** to target personas.

### Business Value

| Stage | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Funnel Position | Top-of-funnel (TOFU) |
| Business Goal | Awareness building among target segments |
| What It Signals | Content visibility within target audience |
| What It Doesn't Signal | Interest, intent, or conversion likelihood |

### Platform-Specific Metrics

#### LinkedIn (Primary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------------|
| **Post Impressions** | Total views of a post | LinkedIn Analytics → Posts → Impressions |
| **Unique Viewers** | Distinct accounts that saw post | LinkedIn Analytics → Unique impressions |
| **Profile Views** | Users who visited profile | LinkedIn → Analytics → Profile views |
| **Discovery Rate** | % impressions from non-followers | LinkedIn → Post details → Discovery |

#### Twitter/X (Secondary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------------|
| **Impressions** | Total times tweet appeared in feeds | Twitter Analytics → Tweet activity |
| **Profile Visits** | Users who clicked through to profile | Twitter Analytics → 28-day summary |
| **Follower Reach %** | % of followers who saw tweet | Impressions / Follower count |

### Target Benchmarks

#### LinkedIn REACH Targets

| Timeframe | Metric | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Month 1** | Avg impressions/post | 500-1,000 | Baseline building phase |
| **Month 3** | Avg impressions/post | 1,500-3,000 | Consistency rewards from algorithm |
| **Month 6** | Avg impressions/post | 3,000-5,000 | Established presence |
| **Month 1** | Profile views/week | 50-100 | Initial curiosity |
| **Month 3** | Profile views/week | 150-300 | Growing reputation |
| **Month 6** | Profile views/week | 300-500 | Thought leadership position |

#### Twitter/X REACH Targets

| Timeframe | Metric | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Month 1** | Avg impressions/post | 200-500 | Limited follower base |
| **Month 3** | Avg impressions/post | 500-1,500 | Cross-pollination from LinkedIn |
| **Month 6** | Avg impressions/post | 1,000-3,000 | Established niche presence |

### Qualified Reach Indicators

**Track not just reach, but WHO you're reaching:**

| Signal | How to Verify | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Job titles in viewers | LinkedIn → Post analytics → Viewer demographics | Target CTO, CPO, VP, PM titles |
| Industry alignment | LinkedIn → Viewer industries | Target SaaS, Enterprise Software, Tech |
| Seniority level | LinkedIn → Viewer seniority | Target Senior+ levels |
| Geographic fit | LinkedIn → Viewer locations | Target Italy, EU, Remote-global |

### Measurement Cadence

| Frequency | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Weekly** | Review top-performing post reach |
| **Monthly** | Compare reach trends month-over-month |
| **Quarterly** | Deep dive into demographic quality of reach |

## REACH Metric #2: ENGAGEMENT (E)

### Definition

**Engagement** measures **quality interactions relative to reach**. Focus on **comments and meaningful interactions**, NOT likes. Comments signal actual interest and conversation potential.

### Business Value

| Stage | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Funnel Position | Middle-of-funnel (MOFU) |
| Business Goal | Content resonance verification |
| What It Signals | Content relevance to audience |
| What It Doesn't Signal | Conversion intent (need CONVERSION metric) |

### Platform-Specific Metrics

#### LinkedIn (Primary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | Weight | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Comments** | Written responses to post | HIGH | LinkedIn → Post → Comments |
| **Comment Quality Score** | 1-5 rating of comment depth | HIGH | Manual review (see rubric) |
| **Reply Rate** | % of comments that receive replies | MEDIUM | Comments with replies / Total comments |
| **Saves** | Users who saved post for later | MEDIUM | LinkedIn → Post details → Saves |
| **Reactions** | All reaction types | LOW | LinkedIn → Post → Reactions |
| **Engagement Rate** | (Comments + Reactions + Shares) / Impressions | MEDIUM | Calculated metric |

#### Twitter/X (Secondary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | Weight | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Replies** | Direct responses to tweet | HIGH | Twitter Analytics → Tweet activity |
| **Quote Tweets** | Retweets with commentary | HIGH | Twitter → Tweet → Quote retweets |
| **Bookmarks** | Users who saved tweet | MEDIUM | Not visible (creator can't see) |
| **Likes** | Heart reactions | LOW | Twitter → Tweet → Likes |
| **Engagement Rate** | (Replies + Retweets + Likes) / Impressions | MEDIUM | Calculated metric |

### Comment Quality Scoring Rubric

**Rate each comment 1-5:**

| Score | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **5 - Exceptional** | Adds new insight, shares experience, asks thoughtful question | "Great point! In my experience at [Company], we found that X works better when combined with Y. Have you tried...?" |
| **4 - Valuable** | Shares personal perspective, meaningful agreement with context | "This resonates. We struggled with exactly this last quarter, and the framework you describe would have saved us 2 weeks." |
| **3 - Engaged** | Shows genuine interest, asks clarifying question | "Interesting approach. How would you apply this to early-stage startups with limited resources?" |
| **2 - Basic** | Simple agreement without depth | "Totally agree!" or "This is so true" |
| **1 - Low Value** | Generic, no real engagement | "Great post!" or emoji only |

**Target: Average comment quality score of 3.5+ by Month 3**

### Target Benchmarks

#### LinkedIn ENGAGEMENT Targets

| Timeframe | Metric | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Month 1** | Comments/post avg | 3-5 | Building initial engagement |
| **Month 3** | Comments/post avg | 8-15 | Consistency pays off |
| **Month 6** | Comments/post avg | 15-30 | Established discussion hub |
| **Month 1** | Engagement rate | 2-3% | LinkedIn average is ~2% |
| **Month 3** | Engagement rate | 4-6% | Above average performance |
| **Month 6** | Engagement rate | 6-10% | Strong niche engagement |
| **Month 1** | Comment quality avg | 2.5 | Early stage |
| **Month 3** | Comment quality avg | 3.5 | Quality community forming |
| **Month 6** | Comment quality avg | 4.0 | High-quality discussions |

#### Twitter/X ENGAGEMENT Targets

| Timeframe | Metric | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Month 1** | Replies/post avg | 1-2 | Lower engagement expected |
| **Month 3** | Replies/post avg | 3-5 | Cross-platform momentum |
| **Month 6** | Replies/post avg | 5-10 | Niche community established |
| **Month 1** | Engagement rate | 1-2% | Twitter averages lower |
| **Month 3** | Engagement rate | 2-4% | Growing presence |

### Engagement Quality Indicators

| Indicator | What It Signals | How to Track |
|-----------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Comment Length** | Depth of engagement | Manual: Avg words per comment |
| **Question Comments** | Interest in topic | Manual: Count "?" comments |
| **Experience Shares** | Relatability | Manual: Comments with "I/we/my" |
| **Tagging Others** | Network expansion | LinkedIn: @ mentions in comments |
| **Return Commenters** | Community building | Manual: Track repeat names |

### Measurement Cadence

| Frequency | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Per Post** | Count comments, note quality examples |
| **Weekly** | Calculate engagement rate, identify top posts |
| **Monthly** | Review comment quality trends, identify return commenters |
| **Quarterly** | Deep analysis of engagement patterns, top contributors |

## REACH Metric #3: AMPLIFICATION (A)

### Definition

**Amplification** measures **organic distribution through shares, reposts, and recommendations**. It signals content virality and network effects—when your community spreads your message for you.

### Business Value

| Stage | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Funnel Position | MOFU → TOFU (expansion) |
| Business Goal | Organic reach multiplication |
| What It Signals | Content worth sharing to others |
| What It Doesn't Signal | Quality of amplified reach |

### Platform-Specific Metrics

#### LinkedIn (Primary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | Weight | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Reposts** | Shared to others' feeds without comment | HIGH | LinkedIn → Post → Reposts |
| **Reposts with Comment** | Shared with added perspective | VERY HIGH | LinkedIn → Post → Reposts → With comments |
| **Send to Profile** | Shared via direct message | MEDIUM | Not trackable (estimate from DMs) |
| **Amplification Rate** | Reposts / Impressions | HIGH | Calculated metric |

#### Twitter/X (Secondary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | Weight | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Retweets** | Simple retweets | MEDIUM | Twitter → Tweet → Retweets |
| **Quote Tweets** | Retweets with commentary | HIGH | Twitter → Tweet → Quote tweets |
| **Amplification Rate** | (Retweets + Quotes) / Impressions | HIGH | Calculated metric |

### Target Benchmarks

#### LinkedIn AMPLIFICATION Targets

| Timeframe | Metric | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Month 1** | Reposts/post avg | 1-3 | Most posts won't be shared |
| **Month 3** | Reposts/post avg | 3-8 | Better content finds sharers |
| **Month 6** | Reposts/post avg | 8-15 | Established share-worthy voice |
| **Month 1** | Amplification rate | 0.1-0.3% | Baseline |
| **Month 3** | Amplification rate | 0.3-0.5% | Growing |
| **Month 6** | Amplification rate | 0.5-1.0% | Strong |
| **Viral Threshold** | Single post reposts | 50+ | Indicates breakout content |

#### Twitter/X AMPLIFICATION Targets

| Timeframe | Metric | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Month 1** | Retweets/post avg | 0-2 | Very limited initially |
| **Month 3** | Retweets/post avg | 2-5 | Growing presence |
| **Month 6** | Retweets/post avg | 5-10 | Established niche |

### Amplification Quality Signals

| Signal | Why It Matters | How to Track |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| **Who Reposts** | Quality of amplifiers matters | Review repost profiles manually |
| **Repost Comments** | Why they're sharing | Read commentary on reposts |
| **Network Expansion** | New audiences reached | New followers after viral posts |
| **Industry Shares** | Sector relevance | Check if PM/tech professionals share |

### Content Types That Amplify

Based on B2B content patterns:

| Content Type | Amplification Potential | Why |
|--------------|------------------------|-----|
| **Contrarian Takes** | Very High | "Finally someone said it" sharing |
| **Numbered Lists** | High | Easy to share for reference |
| **Failure Stories** | High | Relatable, vulnerable content |
| **Frameworks** | Very High | Saves/shares for later use |
| **Data-Backed Insights** | High | Credibility-by-association sharing |
| **Questions/Polls** | Medium | Invites network to participate |

### Measurement Cadence

| Frequency | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Per Post** | Note repost count at 24h and 7d |
| **Weekly** | Identify highest-amplification content |
| **Monthly** | Analyze amplifier demographics |
| **Quarterly** | Review content themes that drive amplification |

## REACH Metric #4: CONVERSION (C)

### Definition

**Conversion** measures **actions that move toward business outcomes**: profile visits, website clicks, booking inquiries, and follow requests. This is where social media activity translates to business value.

### Business Value

| Stage | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Funnel Position | Bottom-of-funnel (BOFU) |
| Business Goal | Lead generation and client acquisition |
| What It Signals | Intent to engage with services |
| What It Doesn't Signal | Actual revenue (need CRM for that) |

### Platform-Specific Metrics

#### LinkedIn (Primary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | Weight | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Profile Visits** | Clicks to view full profile | HIGH | LinkedIn → Analytics → Profile views |
| **Website Clicks** | Clicks to selfrules.org | VERY HIGH | LinkedIn → Post details → Link clicks |
| **Connection Requests** | New requests received | MEDIUM | LinkedIn → Network → Requests |
| **DM Inquiries** | Messages about services | VERY HIGH | LinkedIn Messages → Manual count |
| **Booking Clicks** | Clicks to Google Calendar booking | VERY HIGH | Google Analytics + UTM tracking |
| **Profile-to-Click Ratio** | Website clicks / Profile visits | VERY HIGH | Calculated metric |

#### Twitter/X (Secondary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | Weight | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Profile Clicks** | Clicks to Twitter profile | HIGH | Twitter Analytics → Profile visits |
| **Link Clicks** | Clicks to bio link or tweet links | VERY HIGH | Twitter Analytics → Link clicks |
| **Follows** | New followers | MEDIUM | Twitter Analytics → New followers |
| **DM Inquiries** | Direct message inquiries | HIGH | Twitter DMs → Manual count |

### Conversion Funnel Metrics

```
SOCIAL MEDIA CONVERSION FUNNEL
===============================================================================

IMPRESSIONS (1,000)
     │
     ▼ 10% click through
PROFILE VIEWS (100)
     │
     ▼ 5% click website
WEBSITE VISITS (5)
     │
     ▼ 20% view services
SERVICES PAGE (1)
     │
     ▼ 50% click booking
BOOKING PAGE (0.5)
     │
     ▼ 60% complete booking
BOOKING (0.3)

Target: 0.03% impression-to-booking rate (3 bookings per 10,000 impressions)

===============================================================================
```

## REACH Metric #5: HOLDING (H)

### Definition

**Holding** measures **sustained attention and retention**: dwell time on content, return visitors, follower retention, and ongoing engagement. It signals content quality and community loyalty.

### Business Value

| Stage | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Funnel Position | Retention/Loyalty |
| Business Goal | Community stickiness and repeat engagement |
| What It Signals | Long-term relationship building |
| What It Doesn't Signal | Immediate conversion (lagging indicator) |

### Platform-Specific Metrics

#### LinkedIn (Primary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | Weight | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Dwell Time** | Avg time spent reading post | HIGH | Not directly available (estimate from length/engagement) |
| **Return Commenters** | Users who comment on multiple posts | VERY HIGH | Manual tracking in spreadsheet |
| **Follower Retention** | Followers maintained over time | HIGH | LinkedIn → Followers → Historical data |
| **Newsletter Subscribers** | If enabled, newsletter signups | HIGH | LinkedIn → Newsletter → Subscribers |
| **Post Saves** | Content saved for later | HIGH | LinkedIn → Post → Saves |
| **Consistent Engagers** | Users engaging weekly | VERY HIGH | Manual: Track top 50 names |

#### Twitter/X (Secondary Platform)

| Metric | Definition | Weight | How to Track |
|--------|------------|--------|--------------|
| **Follower Retention** | Followers kept vs. unfollows | MEDIUM | Twitter Analytics → Audience |
| **Return Engagers** | Users who reply to multiple tweets | HIGH | Manual tracking |
| **Bookmark Rate** | (Not visible to creator) | N/A | Cannot track |

### Target Benchmarks

#### LinkedIn HOLDING Targets

| Timeframe | Metric | Target | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Month 1** | Return commenters | 5-10 | Building initial core |
| **Month 3** | Return commenters | 20-40 | Community forming |
| **Month 6** | Return commenters | 50-100 | Strong community |
| **Month 1** | Follower retention rate | 95%+ | Very low churn expected |
| **Month 3** | Follower retention rate | 97%+ | Quality followers stay |
| **Month 6** | Follower retention rate | 98%+ | Loyal community |

| **Month 3** | Saves/post avg | 5-15 | Framework content drives saves |
