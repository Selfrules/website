**Framework:** Content Calibration Matrix (4-1-1 Rule)

## Executive Summary

This document defines the content mix strategy for selfrules.org's social media presence, applying the **4-1-1 Rule Adaptation** framework. The strategy is optimized for:

- **Primary Platform:** LinkedIn (80% effort allocation)
- **Secondary Platform:** Twitter/X (20% effort allocation)
- **Target Audience:** B2B Decision-Makers, Product Teams, Aspiring PMs
- **Business Model:** Professional services (Consulting, Brainstorming, Mentorship)

### The 4-1-1 Rule Adapted for B2B Professional Services

| Content Type | Original 4-1-1 | selfrules.org Adaptation | Percentage |
|--------------|----------------|--------------------------|------------|
| **Value Posts** | 4 curated | 4 original educational | **60%** |
| **Engagement Posts** | 1 retweet/share | 1 discussion starter | **25%** |
| **Promotional Posts** | 1 self-promotion | 1 service CTA | **15%** |

## Value Posts (60%) - Content Pillars

Value posts are the foundation of the content strategy. They establish authority, provide genuine insights, and create reasons for the audience to follow and trust Mattia.

### Pillar 1: PM Methodology Insights

**Purpose:** Establish thought leadership in Product Management practice

**Target Segments:**
- Decision-Makers seeking expertise validation
- Product Teams looking for practical frameworks
- Aspiring PMs learning methodology

**Content Themes:**

| Theme | Example Topics | LinkedIn Format |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Prioritization Frameworks** | ICE vs RICE scoring, MoSCoW method, Opportunity Scoring | Text post + visual |
| **Roadmap Strategy** | Quarterly planning approaches, stakeholder alignment, "No" diplomacy | Long-form post |
| **Cross-Functional PM** | Managing without authority, influence mapping, RACI alternatives | Carousel |
| **Agile Realities** | Scrum vs Kanban real-world, sprint anti-patterns, velocity myths | Story post |

**Example Post Ideas:**

1. "La roadmap perfetta non esiste. Ecco cosa uso invece." (The perfect roadmap doesn't exist. Here's what I use instead.)
2. "Ho detto 'no' a una feature richiesta dal 60% degli utenti. Ecco perché." (I said 'no' to a feature requested by 60% of users. Here's why.)
3. "5 domande che faccio sempre prima di accettare un requisito nel backlog"
4. "RICE vs ICE: quale framework uso dopo 13 anni da PM"

**Posting Cadence:** 2-3 posts/month

## Engagement Posts (25%) - Content Pillars

Engagement posts create two-way conversation, build community, and increase algorithmic reach. They invite participation and demonstrate accessibility.

### Pillar 5: Questions to PM Community

**Purpose:** Start discussions, gather insights, build network engagement

**Target Segments:**
- All PM professionals (peer-to-peer)
- Decision-Makers (thought leadership positioning)
- Aspiring PMs (accessibility demonstration)

**Content Themes:**

| Theme | Example Topics | LinkedIn Format |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Controversial Opinions** | "Unpopular opinion: [PM take]" → ask for reactions | Question post |
| **Experience Gathering** | "What's your biggest PM challenge this quarter?" | Open question |
| **Best Practice Discovery** | "How do you handle [specific situation]?" | Advice request |
| **Tool/Process Opinions** | "Jira vs Linear vs Notion - what do you use and why?" | Comparison post |

**Example Post Ideas:**

1. "Opinione controversa: la roadmap è morta. Come gestite la pianificazione senza?"
2. "Qual è il vostro più grande errore da PM? Il mio: [share]"
3. "Come dite 'no' agli stakeholder senza bruciare ponti? Condividete le vostre frasi."
4. "Una stanza. Ogni venerdì. 40 minuti. Niente slide. Fatti e soluzioni. Funzionerebbe nel vostro team?"

**Posting Cadence:** 2-3 posts/month

## Platform-Specific Adaptations

### LinkedIn Content Mix

LinkedIn is the PRIMARY platform. All content pillars apply fully.

| Content Type | Posts/Week | Posts/Month | Pillars |
|--------------|------------|-------------|---------|
| Value (60%) | 2-3 | 7-10 | Pillars 1-4 |
| Engagement (25%) | 1 | 3-4 | Pillars 5-8 |
| Promotional (15%) | 0-1 | 2 max | Pillars 9-12 |

**LinkedIn-Specific Considerations:**
- Long-form posts (1300 chars) perform best for value content
- Carousels for visual storytelling and frameworks
- Native polls for engagement (better reach than text-only)
- Articles for evergreen, SEO-optimized content
- Comment engagement is crucial — respond within 2 hours

---

### Twitter/X Content Mix

Twitter/X is SECONDARY. Content is repurposed from LinkedIn with format adaptation.

| Content Type | Posts/Week | Posts/Month | Approach |
|--------------|------------|-------------|----------|
| Value (60%) | 1-2 | 5-6 | Condensed LinkedIn highlights |
| Engagement (25%) | 1 | 3-4 | Quote tweets, discussions |
| Promotional (15%) | 0-1 | 1-2 | Link posts to selfrules.org |

**Twitter/X-Specific Considerations:**
- Shorter format (280 chars) — extract key insights from LinkedIn posts
- Threads for longer content (rarely, high-effort)
- Quote tweets for engagement with PM community
- Hashtags: #ProductManagement #PM #agile (use sparingly)
- Higher frequency but lower effort per post
- Cross-link to LinkedIn posts when appropriate

**Content Repurposing Flow:**
```
LinkedIn Long Post → Twitter Thread OR Single Tweet Summary
LinkedIn Carousel → Twitter Single Insight (link to full carousel)
LinkedIn Poll → Twitter Poll (repurpose question)
LinkedIn CTA → Twitter Bio Link (not individual tweets)
```
