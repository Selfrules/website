## Executive Summary

This document defines a **realistic, sustainable posting cadence** for a solo practitioner managing social media alongside B2B consulting services. The cadence is designed to:

- **Maximize impact** on primary platform (LinkedIn)
- **Minimize burnout** through batching and repurposing

- **Scale incrementally** as systems mature

## Content Batching Strategy

### Why Batch? (The Solo Practitioner's Secret)

Creating content in batches is **2-3x more efficient** than daily creation because:
- Reduces context-switching overhead
- Allows deep focus on writing quality
- Creates buffer for busy consulting weeks
- Enables strategic planning vs. reactive posting

### Batching Framework: "Create Once, Schedule Twice"

```
CONTENT BATCHING WORKFLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MONTHLY BATCH SESSION (2-3 hours, once per month)
├── Review analytics from previous month
├── Select 4 content pillars for the month
├── Draft 8-12 LinkedIn post outlines
└── Output: Month's content themes decided

WEEKLY BATCH SESSION (1-1.5 hours, Sunday evening)
├── Write/polish 3-4 LinkedIn posts
├── Create 1-2 Twitter repurposed posts
├── Schedule all posts for the week
└── Output: Week's content queued

DAILY MICRO-SESSIONS (10-15 min, Mon-Fri)
├── Check notifications & respond to comments
├── Engage with 2-3 PM community posts
└── Output: Engagement maintained, relationships built

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### Recommended Batching Schedule

| When | Activity | Duration | Output |
|------|----------|----------|--------|
| **1st Sunday of Month** | Monthly planning + outline drafting | 2-3 hrs | 8-12 post outlines |
| **Every Sunday** | Weekly writing + scheduling | 1-1.5 hrs | 3-4 posts queued |
| **Mon-Fri Daily** | Comment responses + community engagement | 10-15 min | Engagement maintained |

## Cadence Adjustment Triggers

### When to REDUCE Cadence

| Trigger | Action | Duration |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Heavy consulting week | Drop to 2 posts | 1 week |
| Travel/conferences | Pre-schedule + reduce to 1 post | As needed |
| Personal burnout | Pause posting, maintain engagement only | 1-2 weeks |
| Content quality dropping | Reduce volume, focus on quality | 2 weeks |

### When to INCREASE Cadence

| Trigger | Action | Duration |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Content buffer built (8+ posts ready) | Add 4th weekly post | Ongoing |
| High engagement on recent posts | Capitalize with follow-up posts | 1-2 weeks |
| Launch/event promotion | Temporary increase to 5 posts | 1 week |
| Twitter foundation ready | Add Twitter cadence (2 posts/week) | Ongoing |

## Decision Framework: Capacity Check

Before each week, ask yourself:

```
WEEKLY CAPACITY CHECK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. How many client hours this week?
   □ Heavy (25+ hrs)  → Reduce to 2 posts + engagement only
   □ Normal (15-25 hrs) → Standard 3-4 posts
   □ Light (<15 hrs)  → Standard OR build content buffer

2. Do I have content ready?
   □ Yes, batched → Execute schedule as planned
   □ Partially ready → Write missing posts Sunday
   □ No content ready → Emergency: 2 repurposed posts minimum

3. Energy level?
   □ High → Full cadence + extra engagement
   □ Normal → Standard cadence
   □ Low → Reduce cadence, prioritize rest

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```
