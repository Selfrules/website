## Phase T: TARGET - Define Ideal Community Member

**Goal:** Clearly define who belongs in the community and who doesn't. This clarity enables all subsequent phases to be more effective.

### 3 Primary Community Personas

Based on the target audience profile analysis, the ideal community members are:

#### Persona 1: "The Overwhelmed PM Leader"

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Title** | CTO, CPO, VP Product, Head of Product |
| **Company** | B2B SaaS or Enterprise Software, 50-500 employees |
| **Location** | Italy (primary), EU (secondary), Remote-friendly (tertiary) |
| **Pain Point** | Cross-functional chaos, roadmap overwhelm, alignment theater |
| **Content Need** | Strategic insights, battle-tested frameworks, executive perspective |
| **Community Value** | Peer benchmarking, executive-level discussions, trusted advisor access |
| **Conversion Goal** | Consulting service (€2K-5K sessions) |

**Identification Signals:**
- Posts about leadership challenges, scaling teams, strategic decisions
- Engages with content about organization design, product strategy
- Uses phrases like "alignment," "roadmap chaos," "stakeholder management"
- Shares hiring content for senior PM roles

---

#### Persona 2: "The Execution-Focused PM"

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Title** | Product Manager, Senior PM, Product Owner, Team Lead |
| **Company** | Tech startup or scale-up, 10-200 employees |
| **Location** | Italy, EU, Global remote |
| **Pain Point** | Stakeholder overwhelm, prioritization paralysis, cross-functional friction |
| **Content Need** | Practical how-tos, meeting tactics, prioritization frameworks |
| **Community Value** | Peer support, tactical advice, skill development |
| **Conversion Goal** | Brainstorming service (€500-2K sessions) |

**Identification Signals:**
- Posts about sprint challenges, backlog management, feature prioritization
- Asks tactical questions in comments ("How do you handle...?")
- Shares wins and struggles from day-to-day PM work
- Engages with Agile/Scrum content

---

#### Persona 3: "The Aspiring PM"

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Title** | Junior PM, Associate PM, Designer-to-PM, Developer-to-PM, Project Manager |
| **Company** | Any (often early-stage career at smaller companies) |
| **Location** | Italy, Global (strong English segment) |
| **Pain Point** | Career transition uncertainty, skill gaps, imposter syndrome |
| **Content Need** | Career advice, learning paths, failure stories, "how I did it" |
| **Community Value** | Mentorship access, career guidance, inspirational stories |
| **Conversion Goal** | Mentorship service (€200-500/month) |

**Identification Signals:**
- Posts about career transitions, job searching, interview prep
- Asks questions about PM career paths, certifications, skills
- Engages with "lessons learned" and "failure stories" content
- Follows PM thought leaders and course creators

### Community Exclusion Criteria

**NOT the ideal community member:**

| Type | Why Exclude | How to Identify |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Passive lurkers with no engagement intent | No community contribution | Never comment, just view |
| Job spammers | Distract from value focus | Only post job links, no discussion |
| Aggressive self-promoters | Extract value without giving | Every comment links to their product |
| Non-PM roles with no adjacent interest | Topic mismatch | Engage only to sell unrelated services |

**Boundary Tactics:**
- Focus engagement on commenters who add value to discussions
- Politely redirect off-topic promoters in DMs
- Don't respond to spam or low-effort comments
- Nurture high-quality commenters with personalized responses

**Goal:** Facilitate connections between community members, not just with Mattia. Transform followers into a network.

| # | Tactic | How to Execute | Purpose |

### Discussion Facilitation Framework

**Turning comments into conversations:**

```
DISCUSSION FACILITATION FLOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. ORIGINAL POST (Mattia)
   ↓
2. COMMENT A (Member 1)
   ↓
3. MATTIA REPLY + QUESTION
   "Interessante! Come vedi questo rispetto a [related topic]?"
   ↓
4. COMMENT B (Member 2) responds to A
   ↓
5. CONNECTOR COMMENT (Mattia)
   "Ottimo scambio! @Member1 @Member2 - avete esperienze diverse qui.
    Cosa pensate della terza opzione: [introduce new angle]?"
   ↓
6. EXPANDED THREAD with peer-to-peer dialogue
   ↓
7. FOLLOW-UP POST (if thread is rich enough)
   "La discussione sul mio ultimo post ha generato idee incredibili.
    Ecco il riassunto + i contributi chiave di @Member1 e @Member2..."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### Engagement Post Structures for Bonding

**Question formats that encourage peer-to-peer interaction:**

1. **The "How Do You" Question:**
   > "Come gestite le richieste dell'ultimo minuto dagli stakeholder? Condividete la vostra strategia - sono curioso di vedere quanti approcci diversi emergono."

2. **The "Unpopular Opinion" Debate:**
   > "Opinione controversa: la retrospettiva settimanale è una perdita di tempo per team maturi. Agree or disagree? Taggate qualcuno che ha un'opinione forte su questo."

3. **The "Would You Rather" Scenario:**
   > "Preferiresti: (A) lavorare su un prodotto che ami in un'azienda disfunzionale, o (B) lavorare su un prodotto 'meh' in un team fantastico? Motivate la risposta."

4. **The "Challenge Share" Prompt:**
   > "Questa settimana ho dovuto dire 'no' a un executive per la terza volta. Qual è stata la vostra sfida più difficile questa settimana? Condividiamole e aiutiamoci a vicenda."
