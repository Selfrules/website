After applying the Platform Selection Scorecard methodology to all five candidate platforms, **LinkedIn emerges as the clear primary platform** with a score of 9.0/10, significantly ahead of all other options. ## Scoring Methodology

### Platform Selection Scorecard Framework

| Factor | Question | Weight |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Audience Presence** | Are ideal B2B customers actively using this platform? | 2x |
| **Content Fit** | Does thought leadership content style work here? | 1x |
| **Competitive Gap** | Is there room to stand out vs. other PMs/consultants? | 1x |
| **Resource Match** | Can a solo practitioner consistently create required content? | 1x |
| **Business Alignment** | Does platform support consultation/booking conversions? | 1x |

**Formula:** `(Audience × 2 + Content + Gap + Resources + Alignment) / 7`

**Scoring Scale:** 1-10 (1 = Very Poor, 5 = Average, 10 = Excellent)

**Threshold:** Prioritize platforms scoring 7+. Focus on maximum 2-3 platforms.

### Twitter/X Platform Analysis

**Why Twitter/X Scores Lower:**

2. **Resource Intensity**: Twitter requires more frequent posting (ideally daily) and active engagement (replying, quote-tweeting) to maintain visibility. Algorithm favors consistency.

3. **Conversion Challenge**: Twitter users are in "content consumption" mode, not "purchase" mode. Booking a consultation from a tweet is a longer journey.

4. **Competition Reality**: Global PM Twitter is highly competitive. Breaking through requires exceptional content or niche positioning.

**Conditional Recommendation**: Use Twitter/X for:
- Amplifying LinkedIn content (cross-post insights)
- Engaging with global PM community
- Networking with international prospects
- Testing short-form content ideas

## Platform Scoring: YouTube

### Score: 4.7/10 ❌ NOT RECOMMENDED (NOW)

| Criterion | Score | Weight | Weighted | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Audience Presence** | 5 | 2x | 10 | Moderate presence. PM content exists on YouTube, but it's not where B2B buyers discover consultants. More educational/tutorial audience than purchasing decision-makers. |
| **Content Fit** | 6 | 1x | 6 | Long-form video could work for PM tutorials, case studies, interviews. However, requires significant production shift from current text-based content style. |
| **Competitive Gap** | 5 | 1x | 5 | High competition. YouTube PM space has established creators (Lenny's Podcast, Product School). Italian PM YouTube is underserved but requires significant investment to capture. |
| **Resource Match** | 2 | 1x | 2 | Very high effort. Video production, editing, thumbnails, optimization all require specialized skills and significant time. Not sustainable for solo practitioner without production support. |
| **Business Alignment** | 5 | 1x | 5 | Long funnel to conversion. YouTube builds awareness and authority but doesn't directly drive consultation bookings. Viewers may subscribe but not convert. |

**Calculation:** (5×2 + 6 + 5 + 2 + 5) / 7 = **33 / 7 = 4.7**

### YouTube Platform Analysis

**Why YouTube Is Not Recommended Now:**

1. **Resource Reality**: Video content requires 5-10x the effort of text content. Scripting, filming, editing, thumbnails, SEO optimization - not feasible for a solo practitioner with a full-time PM role.

2. **Competition Barrier**: Established PM YouTubers have years of content library advantage. Breaking through requires exceptional production quality or unique angle.

3. **Conversion Efficiency**: YouTube views → subscriptions → website visits → bookings is a long funnel with high drop-off at each stage.

**Future Consideration**: YouTube could become viable if:
- Mattia hires video production support
- Podcast format is launched (repurpose to YouTube)
- Educational course/product is developed (YouTube as funnel)

### TikTok Platform Analysis

**Why TikTok Is Strongly Not Recommended:**

1. **Complete Audience Mismatch**: TikTok's demographics (Gen Z, entertainment-seekers) have zero overlap with B2B professional services buyers.

2. **Content Format Incompatibility**: 15-60 second videos cannot convey the depth of PM consulting value proposition. Would require oversimplification that undermines expertise.

3. **Unsustainable Cadence**: TikTok algorithm rewards 3-5 posts per day. This is physically impossible alongside a full-time PM role and consulting practice.

4. **Zero Business Case**: There is no realistic path from TikTok viewer → consultation booking for B2B professional services.

**Only Exception**: If targeting Gen Z aspiring PMs exclusively and willing to invest in video production team. Not aligned with current business model.
