**Topic:** "The 'Regret Minimization' Framework for PM prioritization"

**Hook Formula:** "Here's a framework that changed how I [do X]"

**Post Outline:**
```
Every PM knows RICE. Few use it correctly.

After 13 years, here's the framework that actually works: "Regret Minimization."

The problem with RICE/ICE:
❌ Teams game the "Confidence" score
❌ Numbers become political
❌ Features with internal champions always win

The Regret Minimization Filter:

For every feature request, ask ONE question:
"If we don't ship this in 6 months, will we regret it?"

Three possible answers:

1. "YES, absolutely"
   → Top of backlog. No discussion.
   → Usually: customer pain, revenue at risk, competitive threat

2. "NO, not really"
   → Goes to "maybe later" pile
   → Usually: nice-to-have, internal requests, vanity features

3. "It depends"
   → Needs more discovery
   → Usually: we don't understand the problem well enough

Why this works:
- Cuts through politics (can't game an emotional question)
- Forces long-term thinking
- Creates clear decisions instead of endless scoring debates

I've used this to cut 60% of backlogs in strategic planning sessions.

What's YOUR prioritization framework that actually works?

—

#ProductManagement #prioritization #PM #framework
```
