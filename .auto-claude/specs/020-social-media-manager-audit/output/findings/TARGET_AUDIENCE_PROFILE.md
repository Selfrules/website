**Subject:** Mattia Filippo De Luca (selfrules.org)

**Auditor:** Social Media Strategy Audit - Phase 1
**Subtask:** 1-3 - Analyze target audience profile from website positioning

---

## Executive Summary

Based on analysis of selfrules.org website positioning, service offerings, brand messaging, and professional background, the target audience for Mattia's social media strategy consists of **four distinct segments**:

1. **Decision-Makers at B2B Companies** - seeking strategic PM consulting
2. **Product Teams/Leaders** - needing facilitation and alignment
3. **Aspiring & Junior PMs** - seeking career mentorship
4. **Recruiters & Hiring Managers** - for potential employment opportunities

All segments share a common characteristic: they need someone who can **translate between business, design, and technology** - Mattia's core value proposition.

## 1. Primary Target Segment: Decision-Makers at B2B Companies

**Decision Triggers:**
- Missed deadlines on key initiatives
- High turnover or burnout in product/dev teams

- Board/investor pressure on product delivery

## Pain Points Summary (Cross-Segment)

### Universal Pain Points (All Audiences)

1. **Cross-Functional Miscommunication**
   - Designer speaks "user journey", developer speaks "technical debt", business speaks "revenue"
   - Need: Someone who translates between all three

2. **Prioritization Paralysis**
   - Too many features, unclear priorities
   - "Urgent" vs "Important" confusion

3. **Alignment Theater**
   - Everyone agrees in meetings, nothing happens afterward
   - Consensus without clarity

4. **Execution Bottlenecks**
   - Multiple projects in progress, none finishing
   - Context switching killing productivity

### Segment-Specific Pain Points

| Segment | Primary Pain Point | Secondary Pain Point |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Decision-Makers | Strategic alignment across teams | Time to value/market |
| Product Teams | Stakeholder management overload | Team collaboration dysfunction |
| Aspiring PMs | Career transition uncertainty | Skill gap awareness |
| Recruiters | Finding T-shaped PM talent | Assessing soft skills remotely |
