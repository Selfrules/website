# First Impression Audit - selfrules.org

**Audit Date:** 2026-01-26
**Methodology:** Art Director 5-Second First Impression Test
**URLs Analyzed:** https://selfrules.org (IT) | https://selfrules.org/en (EN)

## 5-Second First Impression Test Results

### 1. What Grabs Attention First?

**Primary Attention Grabber:** The bold, multi-line headline dominates the viewport with its unconventional line breaks and direct, conversational copy.

**IT Version:** "Il PM che chiami quando tutti dicono 'sì' ma nessuno sa cosa fare"
**EN Version:** "The PM you call when everyone says 'yes' but nobody knows what to do"

The headline immediately differentiates from generic portfolio sites by addressing a specific pain point rather than listing skills.

**Secondary Elements:**
- Badge system: "UX • CODE • PM" positioning immediately above headline
- Failure narrative: "I failed as a designer. Then as a developer." creates intrigue
- Navigation simplicity: Only 3 items (Home, Work Together, Let's Talk)

### 2. Emotional Response Triggered

| Emotion | Intensity (1-5) | Description |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|
| Recognition | 5/5 | Visitor immediately identifies with described problem |
| Relief | 4/5 | "Finally someone who gets it" response |
| Trust | 4/5 | Vulnerability through admitting failures builds credibility |
| Curiosity | 4/5 | "How did failures become expertise?" |
| Respect | 3/5 | 13 years experience implies wisdom |

**Overall Emotional Arc:** The site triggers a problem-solution recognition pattern. The visitor feels seen before they feel sold to.

### 3. Brand Memorability Assessment

**Memorability Score: 8/10**

**What Makes It Memorable:**
- **Specificity over genericity:** Owns a very specific positioning ("the PM you call when...")
- **Counter-positioning:** Leads with failures, not achievements
- **Distinctive voice:** Conversational Italian/English that avoids corporate polish
- **Problem-first approach:** Speaks to visitor's pain before introducing the solution

**What Could Be More Memorable:**
- No strong visual brand mark or logo
- Color palette is subtle (brutalist but not distinctive)
- Missing a "signature" visual element that could become iconic

## Key Findings: First Impression Audit

### Strengths

1. **Positioning Clarity:** Within 5 seconds, the visitor understands WHO this is for (teams in chaos) and WHAT value is offered (practical PM expertise)

2. **Emotional Hook:** The failure-first narrative is counterintuitive and memorable - it inverts the typical portfolio humble-brag

3. **Voice Distinctiveness:** Neither corporate nor overly casual - strikes a "pragmatic professional" tone that stands apart

4. **Problem-Solution Framework:** Addresses visitor pain before pitching services - builds trust through empathy

### Opportunities

1. **Visual Brand Mark:** No strong logo or visual signature element - harder to recall visually vs. verbally

2. **Hero CTA Strength:** While navigation is clear, a stronger call-to-action IN the hero section could improve conversion

3. **Color Differentiation:** Brutalist aesthetic is well-executed but common - opportunity to own a more distinctive color combination

4. **First-Fold Impact:** Could benefit from one "hero moment" visual element that becomes signature

### Risks

1. **Over-reliance on Copy:** The brand's strength is verbal - if copy is skimmed, brand impact reduces

2. **Brutalist Fatigue:** As neobrutalism becomes trendy, design may feel less distinctive over time
