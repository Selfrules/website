# Subtask 4-1: Competitive Benchmark Site Identification

**Date**: 2026-01-27
**Phase**: 4 - Competitive Benchmarking
**Status**: Completed

## Objective

Identify 3 comparable portfolio/consulting sites in the PM/Tech sector that serve as appropriate benchmarks for evaluating selfrules.org's UX/UI.

---

## Selection Criteria

Sites were selected based on:
1. **Similar Target Audience**: PMs, startup founders, tech teams, early-career professionals
2. **Similar Positioning**: Individual consultant/portfolio + personal brand (not company sites)
3. **PM/Product Focus**: Established credibility in product management space
4. **Comparable Services**: Consulting, coaching, workshops, content (similar to selfrules.org)
5. **High-Quality UX**: Professional execution worth benchmarking against
6. **Booking/Contact Mechanisms**: Direct conversion paths for consulting services

## Why These 3 Are Appropriate Comparisons

### 1. Audience Alignment
All three sites target product professionals - the same primary audience as selfrules.org. This ensures UX patterns are relevant and transferable.

### 2. Individual Consultant Focus
Unlike agency/company sites (e.g., SVPG), these are all personal brand sites where one individual is the primary offering. This matches selfrules.org's positioning.

### 3. Different Design Philosophies
The three sites represent distinct approaches:
- **Gibson Biddle**: Traditional professional (Squarespace template)
- **Lenny Rachitsky**: Minimal premium (Framer custom)
- **Teresa Torres**: Content-rich hub (WordPress/Ghost)

selfrules.org's neobrutalist approach is **unique** among these, providing opportunity for differentiation analysis.

### 4. Varying Conversion Strategies
- **Calendar booking** (Gibson - calendar availability)
- **External platform** (Lenny - newsletter/podcast)
- **Course enrollment** (Teresa - Product Talk Academy)
- **Direct booking** (selfrules.org - Google Calendar embed)

This variety allows for comprehensive conversion path benchmarking.

### 5. Established Authority
All three competitors are recognized PM community figures:
- Gibson: 30K+ newsletter subscribers
- Lenny: #1 PM newsletter on Substack
- Teresa: 13,000+ students trained

Benchmarking against established authorities ensures high standards for comparison.
