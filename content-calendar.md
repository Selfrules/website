## Publishing Workflow (Solo Founder)

Each content piece follows this streamlined workflow:

```
Day 1: Research + Outline (1h)
  └─ Competitor SERP analysis for target keyword
  └─ Outline: H2/H3 structure, key points, unique angle

Day 2-3: Write + Optimize (3-5h)
  └─ Draft content (1,500-2,500 words depending on type)
  └─ On-page SEO: title, meta desc, headings, keyword placement
  └─ Internal links to/from cluster hub + related content
  └─ Schema markup (Article, FAQ, HowTo as appropriate)

Day 4: Publish + Distribute (1h)
  └─ Publish to site
  └─ Share on LinkedIn (primary distribution channel)
  └─ Submit URL to Google Search Console for indexing
```

**Time Budget:** 5-7 hours/week total (realistic for solo founder with other responsibilities)

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|:----------:|------------|
| Solo founder burnout from 2 posts/week | Medium | Weeks 1-4 are 1 post/week; ramp to 2/week only in Months 2-3. Skip a week if needed — consistency > volume |
| Content quality drops with higher volume | Medium | Use consistent outline template. Focus on personal experience (faster to write, better E-E-A-T) |
| Italian content cannibalization | Low | Separate URL structures (`/it/blog/` vs `/blog/`), proper hreflang tags, distinct keywords per language |
| New pages not indexed | Medium | Submit all URLs via Google Search Console immediately. Ensure sitemap.xml is auto-updated |
| Keyword competition increases | Low | Blue ocean Italian keywords have structural barriers (language). Monitor monthly via Search Console |
