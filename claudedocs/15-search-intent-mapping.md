## Search Intent Categories Defined

| Intent Type | Definition | User Behavior | Content Strategy |
|-------------|------------|---------------|------------------|
| **Informational** | Seeking knowledge, answers, or education | Reading, learning, researching | Blog posts, guides, tutorials, FAQs |
| **Navigational** | Looking for a specific website, brand, or person | Direct search for known entity | Homepage, about page, branded landing pages |
| **Commercial Investigation** | Researching before making a purchase decision | Comparing options, evaluating providers | Comparison guides, case studies, testimonials |
| **Transactional** | Ready to take immediate action (buy, hire, contact) | Clicking CTAs, filling forms, booking | Service pages, pricing, contact forms |
