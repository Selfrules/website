## Executive Summary

This document identifies 10 primary keywords for selfrules.org based on Mattia's unique positioning as a "product manager translator" who bridges business, design, and technology. Keywords were selected to capitalize on the "translator" metaphor that differentiates Mattia from traditional PM consultants.

**Research Methodology:**
- WebSearch analysis of keyword landscape
- Competitor positioning research
- Analysis of existing metadata keywords in `app/layout.tsx`
- Search intent classification based on SERP analysis
- Volume/difficulty estimates based on niche B2B keyword patterns

**Note on Data Sources:** Exact search volumes require paid tools (Ahrefs, SEMrush, Google Keyword Planner with active campaigns). Estimates below are based on:
- Industry benchmarks for B2B professional services keywords
- Italian market sizing (approximately 10-15% of US volumes for English keywords)
- Long-tail keyword patterns typical for niche personal brands

## Detailed Keyword Analysis

### 1. product manager translator

**Volume Estimate:** 50-100/month (global)
**Difficulty:** Low (15-25)
**Search Intent:** Navigational/Commercial Investigation
**Priority:** P1 - Core Brand Keyword

**Rationale:**
- Unique positioning keyword with virtually no direct competition
- Aligns perfectly with Mattia's "translator" metaphor from homepage content
- Previous SEO audit specifically recommended targeting this keyword
- Low difficulty = opportunity to own SERP position 1

**SERP Analysis:**
- Currently dominated by literal translation/localization services
- No personal brand PM sites targeting this exact phrase
- Content gap opportunity: Mattia could be the only PM using this metaphor

**Content Recommendation:**
- Homepage should rank for this (currently integrating)
- Create dedicated "What is a Product Manager Translator?" pillar page
- Blog series: "Translating Between [Business/Design/Tech]"

### 2. technical product manager

**Volume Estimate:** 2,900-5,400/month (global)
**Difficulty:** High (65-75)
**Search Intent:** Informational/Commercial
**Priority:** P2 - High Volume, High Competition

**Rationale:**

## Search Intent Classification Guide

| Intent Type | Definition | Keywords |
|-------------|------------|----------|
| **Informational** | Seeking knowledge/education | technical PM, PM cross-functional, design background, bridge business tech |
| **Navigational** | Looking for specific site/person | product manager translator |
| **Commercial Investigation** | Researching before decision | PM che parla design e codice, PM freelance, PM background tecnico, product strategy pragmatico |
| **Commercial/Transactional** | Ready to hire/buy | consulente product management |
