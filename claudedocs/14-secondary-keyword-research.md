## Executive Summary

This document identifies 10 secondary keywords for selfrules.org, complementing the primary keywords from subtask 4-1. Secondary keywords focus on:
- **Long-tail keywords** (3-5+ word specific phrases)
- **Question-based keywords** (what/how/why queries)
- **Niche variations** (regional, methodological, stage-specific)

These keywords support content strategy by targeting lower-competition search queries where selfrules.org can establish authority quickly.

**Research Methodology:**
- WebSearch analysis of keyword landscape
- SERP intent analysis
- Italian market keyword research
- Competitor gap identification
- Volume/difficulty estimates based on B2B professional services benchmarks
