## Appendix: E-E-A-T Authoritativeness Definition

From Google's Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines:

> "Authoritativeness: Consider the reputation of the content creator and the website. Is the creator known as a leading or authoritative source for this topic?"

### What Google Looks For:

1. **External recognition** from other authoritative sources
2. **Backlinks** from trusted industry websites
3. **Mentions** in industry publications and communities
4. **Social proof** from professional network engagement
5. **Third-party validation** through reviews, testimonials, press

### Why Authoritativeness Matters for Personal Brand:

- Personal brands compete against established industry voices
- Consulting requires demonstrated credibility
- Without external signals, Google has no trust anchor
- Authoritativeness often determines SERP rankings for competitive terms
