## Appendix: E-E-A-T Experience Definition

From Google's Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines:

> "Experience: Consider the extent to which the content creator has the necessary first-hand or life experience for the topic."

### What Google Looks For:

1. **First-hand accounts** of using products, visiting places, doing activities
2. **Personal stories** that demonstrate lived experience
3. **Original content** that couldn't be created without direct experience
4. **Specific details** that only someone with experience would know
5. **Ongoing engagement** with the topic (not one-time content)

### Why Experience Matters for Personal Brand:

- Personal brands are YMYL-adjacent (career advice = financial impact)
- Consulting services require trust in practitioner's background
- Case studies need verifiable outcomes to build credibility
- Ongoing content shows continuous relevance
