## Appendix: E-E-A-T Expertise Definition

From Google's Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines:

> "Expertise: Consider the extent to which the content creator has the necessary knowledge or skill for the topic."

### What Google Looks For:

1. **Formal credentials** appropriate to the topic (degrees, certifications)
2. **Practical knowledge** demonstrated through specific examples
3. **Topic depth** that shows beyond-surface understanding
4. **Consistent focus** on areas of claimed expertise
5. **Professional identity** that matches expertise claims

### Why Expertise Matters for Personal Brand:

- Personal brands are YMYL-adjacent (career/business advice = financial impact)
- Consulting services require trust in practitioner's knowledge
- Credentials need verification for credibility
- Depth of content signals genuine knowledge vs. surface-level claims
