## Executive Summary

Based on comprehensive competitor identification (5-1) and content analysis (5-2), this report identifies **8 competitive gap opportunities** where selfrules.org can win against established competitors. These gaps leverage Mattia's unique positioning as a "product manager translator" with dual technical (4 years dev) and design (4 years UX) backgrounds.

**Key Finding**: The "translator" metaphor represents a **true blue ocean opportunity** with ZERO direct competition. Combined with weak Italian market presence from global fractional PM services, selfrules.org has multiple high-value gaps to exploit.
