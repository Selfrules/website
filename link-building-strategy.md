# Link Building Strategy: selfrules.org

**Audit Date:** 2026-01-27
**Target Audience:** Solo founder (Mattia Filippo De Luca)
**Current Backlink Profile Score:** 8/100
**Estimated Domain Authority:** 5-15/100
**Strategic Goal:** Reach DA 25+ within 12 months to rank for competitive PM keywords

---

## Executive Summary

selfrules.org has a near-zero backlink profile: ~5 referring domains, zero editorial backlinks, and zero content-driven inbound links. The profile is completely clean (no toxic links), which means we're building on a blank canvas rather than cleaning up past mistakes.

This strategy prioritizes **quality over quantity** and **sustainable tactics over quick hacks**. Every tactic is designed for a solo founder spending 3-5 hours per week on link building. The approach follows three phases: Foundation (Months 1-2), Growth (Months 3-6), and Authority (Months 6-12).

**Expected Outcome:** 40-60 referring domains and DA 20-30 within 12 months, sufficient to rank for Tier 1-2 keywords in the keyword strategy.

### Tactic 2: HARO / Connectively Expert Responses

**Why:** High-authority DoFollow backlinks from journalists and bloggers seeking expert quotes. Minimal effort per response, high DA potential (news sites DA 70+).

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Effort** | 2-3 hours/week (monitor + respond to 3-5 queries) |
| **Frequency** | Daily monitoring, 3-5 responses/week |
| **Expected links per month** | 1-3 DoFollow (10-20% success rate) |
| **Time to first result** | 2-6 weeks |
| **Solo founder fit** | Excellent — short bursts, flexible timing |

**How to Execute:**

1. Sign up for [Connectively](https://www.connectively.us/) (HARO successor) as a source
2. Set up alerts for: "product manager", "product management", "startup", "UX design", "tech teams", "agile", "project management"
3. Respond within 2 hours of query (speed = higher selection rate)
4. Include credentials: "Mattia De Luca, Product Manager and founder of selfrules.org, with experience at QubicaAMF and Italy's PM consulting space"
5. Keep responses to 150-300 words, always include a quotable soundbite

**Expert Positioning Angles:**
- PM leadership and team dynamics
- Design-development communication
- Startup product strategy
- Italian/European tech market perspective (differentiator!)
- Career transitions into product management

**Action Steps:**
1. Day 1: Create Connectively profile with "PM translator" positioning
2. Daily: Scan morning digest (5 min), respond to 1-2 relevant queries (20-30 min each)
3. Track responses and published links in a simple spreadsheet

### Tactic 7: Community Engagement & Relationship Building

**Why:** Authentic community participation builds relationships that lead to organic backlink opportunities (guest post invitations, resource page inclusions, co-marketing). This is the "slow burn" tactic that compounds over time.

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Effort** | 2-3 hours/week (commenting, sharing, helping) |
| **Frequency** | Daily micro-interactions (10-15 min) |
| **Expected links per month** | 0-2 (indirect, relationship-driven) |
| **Time to first result** | 2-4 months |
| **Solo founder fit** | Excellent — integrates into daily routine |

**Target Communities:**

| # | Community | Platform | Activity |
|---|-----------|----------|----------|
| 1 | r/productmanagement | Reddit | Answer questions, share insights (no self-promo) |
| 2 | Product-Led Alliance Slack | Slack | Engage in discussions, offer help |
| 3 | Mind the Product Community | Forum | Share expertise, attend events |
| 4 | Italian PM LinkedIn Groups | LinkedIn | Post insights in Italian, engage with peers |
| 5 | Indie Hackers | Web | Share consulting journey, offer PM advice |
| 6 | Designer + Developer subreddits | Reddit | Cross-pollinate PM perspective into design/dev discussions |

**Rules of Engagement:**
1. **80/20 rule:** 80% helping others, 20% mentioning your own work
2. **Never drop links** in first 10 interactions in any community
3. **Add genuine value** — answer questions thoroughly, share frameworks
4. **Natural linking:** When your blog post genuinely answers someone's question, share it
5. **Build relationships** with other content creators for future collaboration

**Action Steps:**
1. Week 1: Join 3-4 communities, set up profiles
2. Daily: 15 min engagement across communities
3. Monthly: Identify 2-3 relationship-building opportunities
4. Quarterly: Evaluate which communities drive the most value

## Anchor Text Strategy

As backlinks grow, aim for this distribution to maintain a natural profile:

| Anchor Type | Target % | Examples |
|-------------|----------|---------|
| Brand/Name | 40-50% | "Selfrules", "Mattia De Luca", "selfrules.org" |
| Keyword-rich | 15-25% | "product manager consultant", "PM translator", "fractional PM" |
| Natural/Descriptive | 15-25% | "this PM blog", "read more about PM consulting" |
| Naked URL | 5-10% | "https://selfrules.org" |
| Generic | 5-10% | "click here", "learn more", "website" |

**Rules:**
- Never use the exact same keyword anchor more than 3 times
- Always vary anchors naturally — let guest post editors choose when possible
- For self-controlled links (directories), use brand anchors
- For earned links (guest posts, HARO), suggest keyword-rich but accept whatever the publisher uses

## Tactics to AVOID (Solo Founder Anti-Patterns)

These tactics are commonly recommended but are **wrong for a solo founder**:

| Tactic | Why to Avoid |
|--------|-------------|
| Mass email outreach campaigns | Low conversion, damages brand, requires automation tools |
| PBN (Private Blog Network) links | Google penalty risk, expensive, unsustainable |
| Paid links / sponsored posts | Against Google guidelines, expensive at scale |
| Blog commenting for links | NoFollow, low value, feels spammy |
| Forum signature links | Outdated, NoFollow, no SEO value |
| Link exchanges ("I'll link you if you link me") | Google penalizes reciprocal link schemes |
| Fiverr/cheap link building services | Toxic links, penalty risk, waste of money |
| Broken link building at scale | Requires enterprise tools (Ahrefs), high effort for low return |
| Infographic submission sites | Outdated tactic, most sites NoFollow now |

## Brand Confusion Mitigation

During backlink research, "The Selfrule Organization" (theselfrule.org, a Web3/blockchain entity) was found to create naming confusion in search results.

**Mitigation in Link Building:**
- Always pair "Selfrules" with "Mattia De Luca" or "Product Manager" in bios and profiles
- Request author bios use: "Mattia De Luca, founder of selfrules.org and product management consultant"
- Avoid standalone "Selfrule" as anchor text (use "Selfrules" with the 's')
- In guest posts, include contextual clues: "selfrules.org, my personal brand site focused on product management"
