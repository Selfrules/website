## Executive Summary

This keyword strategy defines 20 priority keywords for selfrules.org, ranked 1-20 by strategic value. The strategy leverages Mattia's unique "translator" positioning to target blue ocean opportunities in both English and Italian markets. Keywords are classified by search intent (Informational, Navigational, Commercial Investigation, Transactional) and mapped to specific content types for implementation.

**Key Strategic Insights:**
- **40% Italian keywords** (8/20) — underserved market with near-zero competition
- **70% Low/Very Low difficulty** (14/20) — achievable rankings for a new domain
- **50% Commercial+ intent** (10/20) — balanced between lead generation and authority building
- **"Product Manager Translator"** is a verified blue ocean keyword with zero PM competitors

## Data Methodology & Limitations

### Data Sources
- **WebSearch analysis** — SERP competition assessment and competitor identification
- **Industry benchmarks** — B2B professional services keyword volume patterns
- **Italian market sizing** — Approximately 10-15% of US volumes for equivalent English keywords
- **Long-tail patterns** — Niche personal brand keyword typical distributions

### Limitations
- Volume estimates are **ranges** based on benchmarks, not exact tool data (no Ahrefs/SEMrush access)
- Italian market volumes are **particularly uncertain** due to limited public data
- Difficulty scores are **relative estimates** based on SERP analysis, not proprietary algorithms
- No Google Search Console access for **actual impression/click data**

### Recommended Validation
1. **Google Keyword Planner** — Free with Google Ads account (exact volumes)
2. **Ubersuggest** — Free tier for basic keyword metrics
3. **Google Search Console** — Track actual performance post-implementation
4. **Google Trends** — Validate relative interest and seasonal patterns
