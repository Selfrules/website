## FRAMEWORK DEFINITIONS

### AIDA (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)

| Stage | Description | Score Criteria |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Attention** | Does the opening hook grab immediate attention? | 9-10: Impossible to ignore; 7-8: Strong hook; 5-6: Decent but not compelling; 3-4: Weak; 1-2: No hook |
| **Interest** | Does the copy build interest with relevant benefits? | 9-10: Can't stop reading; 7-8: Engaged; 5-6: Mildly interested; 3-4: Losing interest; 1-2: Bored |
| **Desire** | Does the copy create desire for the solution? | 9-10: "I need this now"; 7-8: "I want this"; 5-6: "Might be nice"; 3-4: "Meh"; 1-2: "Not for me" |
| **Action** | Is there a clear, compelling call-to-action? | 9-10: Immediate action; 7-8: Clear path; 5-6: Vague; 3-4: Confusing; 1-2: No CTA |

### PAS (Problem → Agitation → Solution)

| Stage | Description | Score Criteria |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Problem** | Is the problem clearly identified? | 9-10: "That's exactly my problem!"; 7-8: Relatable; 5-6: Generic; 3-4: Unclear; 1-2: No problem stated |
| **Agitation** | Is the problem amplified to create urgency? | 9-10: Urgent pain; 7-8: Increased stakes; 5-6: Mild tension; 3-4: No amplification; 1-2: Minimizes problem |
| **Solution** | Is the solution clearly presented as the answer? | 9-10: Perfect fit; 7-8: Good match; 5-6: Partial solution; 3-4: Weak; 1-2: No solution |

### FAB (Features → Advantages → Benefits)

| Stage | Description | Score Criteria |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Features** | Are specific features/deliverables stated? | 9-10: Crystal clear; 7-8: Good detail; 5-6: Vague; 3-4: Missing; 1-2: None |
| **Advantages** | Are competitive advantages explained? | 9-10: Unique differentiators; 7-8: Some differentiation; 5-6: Generic; 3-4: Weak; 1-2: None |
| **Benefits** | Are customer benefits clearly articulated? | 9-10: Transformation clear; 7-8: Good benefits; 5-6: Features masquerading as benefits; 3-4: Weak; 1-2: None |

## PERSUASION FLOW GAPS IDENTIFIED

### Gap 1: Attention Drops at Contact Section 🔴

**Location:** Contact/Parliamo headline
**Issue:** "Pronto per il prossimo capitolo? Costruiamo qualcosa di straordinario insieme." breaks from the pragmatic, provocative tone established earlier. "Straordinario" (extraordinary) is aspirational fluff that violates Romei pragmatism.
**Impact:** 4-point AIDA Attention drop from Homepage (10) to Contact (6)
**Fix:** Replace with problem-focused hook: "Il tuo prossimo sprint senza caos?"

---

### Gap 2: Newsletter CTA Completely Broken 🔴

**Location:** Footer newsletter signup
**Issue:** "Iscriviti →" offers zero value proposition, zero content promise, zero differentiation.
**Impact:** 13/40 AIDA, 3/30 PAS, 4/30 FAB - catastrophically low across all frameworks
**Fix:** "1 insight a settimana: errori di PM che non farai tu. Zero spam. →"

---

### Gap 3: Agitation Weakness in Service Tiers ⚠️

**Location:** Service tier problem statements
**Issue:** All three tiers identify the problem well but don't amplify the cost of inaction.
**Impact:** Agitation scores of 7-9/10 across tiers - good but not urgent
**Fix:** Add consequence statement to each tier:
- Tier 1: "Ogni settimana senza priorità = features che nessuno usa."
- Tier 2: "Stakeholder allineati oggi = 3 mesi recuperati domani."
- Tier 3: "Ogni progetto che ripete il caos ti costa 6 mesi di carriera."

---

### Gap 4: Action Stage Fragmentation ⚠️

**Location:** Multiple CTAs throughout site
**Issue:** 6+ different CTA variations ("Parliamone", "Prenota una call", "Prenota 15 minuti", "Prenota ora →", "Iscriviti →") create decision fatigue.
**Impact:** Action scores of 7-9/10 - solid but not optimal
**Fix:** Consolidate to 2 primary CTAs:
1. "Parliamone - 15 minuti" (warm + specific)
2. "Prenota ora →" (urgent variant for return visitors)

---

### Gap 5: English Version Persuasion Loss ⚠️

**Location:** English translation throughout
**Issue:** Multiple persuasion elements weakened in translation:
- Missing "Senza traduttori in mezzo" (core differentiator)
- Directive CTAs instead of collaborative
- Less idiomatic agitation language
**Impact:** -4 points average across AIDA, -4 points across PAS
**Fix:**
1. Add "Without translators in between" to English value prop
2. Use "Let's book" instead of "Book"
3. Adapt idioms for English emotional impact

---

### Gap 6: Post-Call Benefit Missing ⚠️

**Location:** Contact section FAB
**Issue:** Benefits stop at "come sbloccarlo" (how to unblock). What happens after the 15-minute call is unclear.
**Impact:** FAB Benefits score drops to 7/10 at conversion point
**Fix:** Add outcome statement: "15 minuti oggi → decisione chiara lunedì. Zero stress."
