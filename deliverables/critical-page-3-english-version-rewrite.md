# Critical Page #3 Rewrite: English Version (Full Homepage)

**Task:** Subtask 3-3 - Critical Page Rewrites
**Agent:** copywriter-hybrid
**Date:** 2026-01-27

---

## Executive Summary

The English version of selfrules.org systematically loses **-2.5 points per section** compared to the Italian version. This isn't a translation problem - it's a localization failure. The Italian version is written with **native fluency and warmth**; the English version reads like a competent but cold translation.

**Critical missing element:** "Senza traduttori in mezzo" (Without translators in between) - the core brand differentiator - is completely absent from the English value proposition.

This rewrite restores parity by:
1. Adding the missing differentiator
2. Converting directive CTAs to collaborative invitations
3. Adapting (not translating) idiomatic expressions
4. Matching Italian warmth with culturally-equivalent English expressions

---

## Voice Calibration Strategy

### Approach: Match Italian Ratios Per Section

Unlike the Newsletter (single calibration) or Contact (conversion page calibration), the English homepage requires **section-by-section voice matching** to restore parity with the Italian version's native fluency.

| Section Type | Voice Calibration | Rationale |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Hero** | 40% Romei / 30% Sinek / 30% Toon | Hook with challenge/question, vulnerability-led narrative |
| **Journey/Value Prop** | 60% Romei / 25% Toon / 15% Sinek | Clear, direct, outcome-focused positioning |
| **Services** | 60% Romei / 25% Toon / 15% Sinek | Project descriptions = outcome-focused |
| **CTAs** | 70% Romei / 20% Toon / 10% Sinek | Ultra-short, action verbs |
| **Testimonials** | 35% Romei / 20% Toon / 45% Sinek | Purpose-driven narrative, story > statistics |
| **Footer/Bio** | 30% Romei / 50% Toon / 20% Sinek | Authentic, credible, relatable |

## Why These Voice Proportions Were Chosen

### Rationale for Section-by-Section Approach

Unlike the Newsletter CTA (single element, one calibration) or Contact Section (conversion-focused, one calibration), the English homepage is a **full-page localization** requiring:

1. **Hero (40/30/30):** The failure narrative requires equal Sinek purpose and Toon vulnerability. Pure Romei would sound cold; pure Toon would undermine authority.

2. **Journey/Value Prop (60/25/15):** The translator positioning is a **definitive stance** - it needs maximum Romei clarity. But the "Chaos." and "pieces" language adds Toon personality.

3. **Services (60/25/15):** Project descriptions per copywriter-hybrid spec. Outcome-focused requires Romei majority.

4. **CTAs (70/20/10):** Standard CTA calibration - ultra-short, action verbs. The 20% Toon comes from "Let's" instead of directive verbs.

5. **Testimonials (35/20/45):** These are mini case studies - Sinek-heavy per spec. The transformation stories connect to purpose.

6. **Footer (30/50/20):** Bio/About calibration - authentic and relatable. The procrastination call-out is pure Toon warmth.
