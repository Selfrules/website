## SCORING RUBRIC REFERENCE

| Score | Clear | Concise | Compelling | Credible |
|-------|-------|---------|------------|----------|
| 9-10 | Instant understanding, no jargon, concrete | Every word essential, tight prose | Strong desire, urgent want | Specific proof, third-party validation |
| 7-8 | Mostly clear, minor complexity | Minor redundancies | Interested, would consider | Good evidence, some specifics |
| 5-6 | Requires re-reading, some jargon | Could cut 20-30% | Mild interest | Claims without proof |
| 3-4 | Confusing structure, industry speak | Verbose, repetitive | Neutral, no pull | Overclaiming, sounds salesy |
| 1-2 | Incomprehensible without context | Could cut 50%+ | Active disinterest | Unbelievable claims |

## HOMEPAGE - ITALIAN VERSION (Primary)

### 1. Hero Headline

**Copy:** "Ho fallito come designer. Poi come developer. Ora sono il PM che chiami quando tutti dicono 'sì' ma nessuno sa cosa fare."

| Criterion | Score | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **Clear** | 9 | Instant understanding. Failure narrative immediately grabs attention. Role clarity (PM). Specific scenario (everyone says yes, no one knows what to do). |
| **Concise** | 9 | Every word earns its place. Short, punchy clauses. No filler. Could not cut without losing meaning. |
| **Compelling** | 10 | Vulnerability hook (failure) + curiosity gap (how did failure lead to expertise?) + relatable pain point (team dysfunction). Urgent desire to learn more. |
| **Credible** | 8 | Specific career progression (designer → developer → PM). "13 years" mentioned in subheadline adds tenure. Would score higher with specific outcomes/numbers. |

**Composite Score: 36/40**
**Weakest C:** Credible (missing quantified outcomes)
**Recommendation:** Add a specific result metric (e.g., "helped 50+ teams...")
