## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

selfrules.org scores **B+ (83.4%)** overall — exceptional clarity and conciseness driven by a masterful "trilingual PM" positioning, but held back by a systematic credibility gap, a catastrophically broken Newsletter CTA (F grade across all frameworks), and an English version that loses 2.5 points per section versus the Italian original. Three targeted fixes — Newsletter CTA rewrite, Contact headline realignment, and English localization restoration — would lift the site from B+ to A- with minimal effort.

## CRITICAL ISSUES

### Issue #1: Newsletter CTA — Catastrophic Failure 🔴

**Current:**
> "Iscriviti →"

**Scores:** AIDA 13/40 (F) | PAS 3/30 (F) | FAB 4/30 (F) | 4 C's 22/30 (D)

**Why it's broken:** Zero value proposition, zero content promise, zero frequency, zero differentiation. The ONLY element on the entire site that doesn't follow the Romei pragmatism pattern.

**Recommended rewrite:**
> **PM pattern che non impari dagli altri.**
> "La maggior parte delle newsletter parla di teoria. Questa parla di errori veri. I miei. Quelli dei 87 progetti che ho gestito. Una email a settimana. 3 minuti di lettura."
> **[Ricevi il primo pattern ora →]**
> "Disiscrizione in 1 click. Zero spam. Solo roba utile."

**Expected impact:** F → B+ (+21 points AIDA, +22 points PAS, +21 points FAB)

---

### Issue #2: Contact Headline — Attention Crash at Conversion Point 🔴

**Current:**
> "Pronto per il prossimo capitolo? Costruiamo qualcosa di straordinario insieme."

**Scores:** AIDA Attention 6/10 (D) — drops from hero's 10/10

**Why it's broken:** "Straordinario" is aspirational fluff that violates Romei pragmatism. Two competing headlines dilute the message. Momentum crashes exactly where it needs to peak.

**Recommended rewrite:**
> **Il tuo prossimo sprint senza caos.**
> "15 minuti per sbloccare quello che sta fermo da settimane."
> [existing supporting copy — KEEP AS-IS]
> **Dopo la call: una decisione chiara e il primo passo da fare domani mattina.**
> **[Prenotiamo 15 minuti →]**

**Expected impact:** AIDA Attention 6→9 (+50%), 4 C's 30→37 (+7 pts), Grade B→A-

---

### Issue #3: English Version — Missing Core Differentiator 🔴

**Current (English value prop):**
> "I don't sell hours. I don't sell consulting. I sell this: a solution your designer understands, your developer can build, and your CFO approves."

**Missing:** "Senza traduttori in mezzo" = "Without translators in between" — the signature line that seals the translator positioning is COMPLETELY ABSENT from English.

**Recommended rewrite:**
> "I don't sell hours. I don't sell consulting.
> **I sell this:** a solution your designer understands, your developer can build, and your CFO approves.
> **Without translators in between.**"

**Expected impact:** +3 points immediately. English parity restored (-2.5 gap → 0).

---

### Issue #4: Credibility Gap — Systemic 🟠

**Current:** 11/15 sections have "Credible" as weakest C. Average Credible score: 7.3/10.

**Why it matters:** Claims are excellent but proof is siloed in testimonials section. No inline social proof near value propositions.

**Recommended fix:** Interleave testimonial snippets with value propositions. Add "87 progetti" or "50+ team" metric to hero. Add mini case study callouts to service tiers.

---

### Issue #5: English CTAs — Directive vs. Collaborative 🟠

**Current:**
> Italian: "Prenotiamo" (Let's book — collaborative)
> English: "Book" (directive — cold)

**Recommended fix:** Change all English CTAs from "Book" → "Let's book"
