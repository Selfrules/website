## Visual Hierarchy Levels Defined

| Level | Purpose | Weight | Example |
|-------|---------|--------|---------|
| **Dominant** | Primary attention capture | Highest | Hero headline |
| **Sub-dominant** | Secondary narrative flow | High | Section titles |
| **Supporting** | Action enablement | Medium-High | CTA buttons |
| **Accent** | Category/type signaling | Medium | Badges |
| **Decorative** | Atmosphere/brand feeling | Low | Floating shapes |
| **Subordinate** | Detail/context provision | Low | Body text |
| **Navigation** | Wayfinding | Minimal | Header links |

## Conclusion

The visual hierarchy of selfrules.org is **functionally strong** with a clear primary focal point (hero headline) that passes the 3-second test. Typography and section structure are excellent. The primary weaknesses are:

1. **Decorative element opacity inconsistency** - Hero shapes at 0.70-0.80 compete with content
2. **Equal-weight card problem** - Multiple sections present options without visual priority
3. **Missing brand visual anchor** - No memorable visual mark for brand recall

Addressing the P1 items (opacity reduction, border-radius fix) provides immediate improvement with minimal effort. The P3 brand visual mark is the transformational change needed for true brand memorability.
