## Document Purpose

This document reconstructs the brand positioning statement for selfrules.org using the standard Branding Consultant template. The positioning statement synthesizes all audit findings from Phases 1-4 to articulate who Mattia serves, what need he addresses, how he frames his offering, what benefit he delivers, and why clients should believe him.

**Template Used:**
```
For [TARGET AUDIENCE] who [NEED/OPPORTUNITY],
[BRAND] is the [CATEGORY/FRAME OF REFERENCE]
That [KEY BENEFIT/PROMISE] because [REASON TO BELIEVE/PROOF]
```

## Section 1: The Reconstructed Positioning Statement

### 1.1 Full Positioning Statement

> **For** overwhelmed decision-makers (CTOs, VPs of Product, Project Directors) at growth-stage companies
> **who** have cross-functional teams that speak different languages—where designers, developers, and business stakeholders say "yes" but nothing ships—
> **Mattia (selfrules)** is the translator PM
> **That** brings clarity to chaos and delivers solutions everyone can understand, build, and approve
> **Because** 13 years of failing as a designer, then as a developer, then as a PM taught him to speak all three languages fluently—and his failures are his proof.

### 1.2 Condensed Version (Elevator Pitch)

> **For** teams in chaos **who** have a communication gap between design, development, and business,
> **Mattia** is the translator PM
> **That** ships solutions everyone understands
> **Because** 13 years of failures across three disciplines made him fluent in all three.

### 1.3 One-Line Version (Tagline)

> "I bridge the gap between design, code, and business—and I have the failures to prove it."

## Section 7: Positioning Vulnerabilities

### 7.1 Potential Weaknesses

| Vulnerability | Risk Level | Mitigation |
|---------------|------------|------------|
| **No visible proof** | HIGH | Add testimonials, case visuals |
| **"Translator" may feel soft** | MEDIUM | Add "ships in weeks" outcome proof |
| **Failure narrative may backfire** | LOW | Photo humanizes, prevents "loser" perception |
| **Neobrutalism contradicts accessibility** | HIGH | Soften visual treatment |

### 7.2 Competitive Threats

| Threat | Likelihood | Counter-Strategy |
|--------|------------|------------------|
| Other PMs claim "translator" | MEDIUM | Double down on failure narrative (unique) |
| Agencies offer similar services | HIGH | Human positioning vs. corporate alternative |
| AI tools reduce PM need | LOW | "Human understanding" can't be automated |
